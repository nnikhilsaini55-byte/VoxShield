SAFETY PROTOCOL / RECOMMENDED ACTIONS

What to do if you encounter pressure tactics

Action Guide

Follow these standardized cybersecurity rules whenever an unexpected caller demands money, credentials, or secrecy.

🛑

1. Pause before transferring

Scammers create artificial panic to bypass your critical thinking. Never initiate instant UPI, wire, or card transfers under pressure.

🔒

2. Never share OTPs or PINs

No genuine police officer, CBI/ED official, bank manager, or judge will ever ask for your OTP, CVV, or banking passwords.

📞

3. Hang up & verify independently

Terminate the call. Look up the organization's official verified contact details on their official website or a physical bank card.

👥

4. Consult a trusted family member

Speak to a family member or friend on a known phone number. Scammers demand secrecy specifically so nobody alerts you.

📱

5. Never install remote-access apps

Refuse instructions to install AnyDesk, TeamViewer, RustDesk, or unknown APK files. These grant fraudsters complete control of your screen.

📝

6. Preserve evidence & report

Take screenshots and note the caller's phone numbers. In India, report cyber fraud immediately to National Helpline 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in.
